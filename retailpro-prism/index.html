Retail Pro Prism

Build data-driven operations with Retail Pro — the freedom of choice, with a lighter, faster, entirely customizable POS platform, unifying data across channels, regions and the cloud. Over 35 years in specialty retail, deployed and supported by Integrated Retail.

Book a Demo
One Software, Every Scale

From a single store to 250+ store enterprises

Global Enterprise POS

For large-scale, multi-national retail operations with complex tax, currency and language requirements.

Chain Store POS

Built for multi-location retailers who need consistent operations across every store.

SMB-POS

Tailored for independent and single-store retailers who still want enterprise-grade tools.

Your Platform for Unifying Data

Fully customizable, integrable software

One software to support all channels & devices — unify and improve efficiency in inventory, customer and store operations management across channels, regions and the cloud. Mobile-first on iOS, Android and Windows.

POS & Checkout

  • Quick and effective POS features, touch-screen optimised
  • Lookup items by barcode or description
  • Flexible customer lookup
  • Issue & track gift certificates, gift cards and store credits
  • Integrated with major EFT payment processing
  • Promotions like BOGO, BTGT, BOOM & coupons

Customer Loyalty

  • Run contact and promotion campaigns via email, SMS and POS notifications
  • Segment customers by demographics, behaviour and geography
  • Analyse customer history

Inventory Management

  • Multi-company inventory tracking across the full product lifecycle
  • Unlimited inventory with images
  • Quick view of stock movement, stock take by zone
  • Unlimited user-defined fields and barcodes per product

E-Commerce Enabled

  • Near real-time inventory, customers and orders
  • Syncs with Magento, OpenCart, Miva, WooCommerce
  • Integrated with a universal order management system

ERP Integrated

  • Integrated with SAP®, ACCPAC®, Microsoft Navision®
  • Open APIs available for most ERPs

Special Order & Gift Registration

  • Track special orders, backorders and unlimited deposits
  • Convert sales to advance bookings/orders
  • Manage gift registries across all stores or one store

Purchasing & Vendor Management

  • Auto PO creation based on min/max stock levels
  • Optimise replenishment via transfer of overstocked or low-selling items
  • Drop-ship and marked-for POs per vendor requirement

Reporting & Productivity

  • 150+ prebuilt reports plus personalised report generation
  • Multi-tier employee commission tracking and sales targets
  • Multi-country, multi-currency, multi-company support
