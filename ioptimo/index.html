Stuck to Sold!

iOptimo helps retail teams forecast demand, spot shortage risk, find excess inventory, and decide which units should move between stores — reducing overstock, preventing stockouts, and moving inventory where it sells best.

From Overstock to Opportunity

One store overstocked. Another out of stock. Same inventory, wrong place.

iOptimo spots the mismatch automatically — placing every product on a live sales-vs-stock quadrant so planners know at a glance what to buy, push, markdown, or redistribute.

Platform

One operating view for forecast, inventory, and transfers

Retail planning breaks when every team works from a different file. iOptimo connects the moving parts so planners can see what’s at risk and what action comes next.

Demand Forecasting

Forecast demand by store, product, size and color so planners can see risk before the selling floor feels it.

Transfer Planning

Find which stores have excess, which stores will run short, and what units should move today.

Inventory Analytics

Track stock cover, slow movers, broken size runs, excess inventory, and replenishment pressure in one place.

AI Assistant

Workflow

From connected data to recommended action

iOptimo turns retail data into prioritised planning decisions — your team moves from integrated inputs to recommended transfers in a single connected system, without switching between tools.

  1. Connect retail data — Integrate sales, inventory, product master, and store records.
  2. Forecast demand — Model expected sell-through by SKU and store.
  3. Detect risk — Find shortages, excess, broken assortments, and unusual demand.
  4. Recommend action — Review transfer plans, priorities, and expected impact.
