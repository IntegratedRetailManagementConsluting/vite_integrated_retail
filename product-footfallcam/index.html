FootfallCam

Every store decision — staffing, layout, marketing spend — is a guess without visitor data. FootfallCam turns foot traffic into the same kind of hard numbers you already track for sales, using privacy-first, edge-processed people-counting sensors and the V9 analytics platform, deployed and supported locally by Integrated Retail.

Book a Demo
Bi-Directional People Counting

A unified platform for business optimisation

Entrance counting, area occupancy and dwell-time analysis, all processed at the edge so no personal data ever leaves the sensor — giving stakeholders privacy-safe, actionable insight into how people actually move through a space.

Every visitor, counted and timestamped — automatically

Marketing ROI & Queue Tracker

  • Checkout throughput monitoring
Your Store, Quantified

One dashboard for footfall, conversion and dwell time

Live visitor counts, conversion rate against transactions, average dwell time, and peak-hour patterns — the same data discipline you already apply to sales, now applied to the people walking past and through your stores.

Privacy by Design

Accurate counting, GDPR-aligned by default

Edge Processing

Counting happens on-device, at the sensor — no faces, images or personal identifiers are transmitted or stored centrally.

Staff Exclusion

Configurable zones and logic filter out staff movement so reported footfall reflects genuine customer traffic.

Modular App Store

Turn on only the applications each site needs — from a single counter to a full multi-country retail network.

Built Per Industry

One hardware + software platform, priced the way you plan stores

Every FootfallCam deployment pairs the sensor hardware with software tuned to your vertical — fashion, F&B, malls, groceries — so the dashboard shows the metrics that matter to that business, not a generic count. And because Pro3™ and Centroid can connect to multiple camera types, including the CCTV you already have installed, most sites don’t need a full hardware refresh to get started — which is what keeps the cost per store, or per square foot, down.

Dedicated per industry

Hardware and software tuned to your vertical — from fashion floors to F&B queues to mall common areas.

Works with what you already have

Pro3™ and Centroid connect to multiple camera types — including your existing CCTV — so most sites skip a full hardware refresh.

Priced per store, not per feature

Cost scales with your footprint — per store or per square foot — instead of a flat, one-size-fits-all licence.

Hardware & Software

The core lineup

AI Box

Pro3™ AI Box

Edge AI processing unit that connects to multiple camera and sensor feeds — including your existing CCTV — and powers Centroid’s analytics.

Sensor

Pro1™ Dome & Fisheye

Compact dome and fisheye sensors for entrances, aisles and single-camera zones — the lower-cost way to start counting per site.

Smart NVR

Centroid

Hybrid DVR that plugs straight into your existing analogue and IP CCTV, adding FootfallCam’s AI analytics without replacing the cameras you already have.

Software Platform

Analytics ManagerV9™

The unified dashboard every sensor and camera feeds into — one login, tuned to your industry’s KPIs, across every store.
