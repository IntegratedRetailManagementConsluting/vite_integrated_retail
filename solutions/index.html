Why We're Different

What sets Integrated Retail apart

Retail technology vendors can install software. A systems integrator with real retail-operations depth does something different — it stays accountable for the outcome.

Specialty retail depth, not generic IT

We focus on fashion, beauty, F&B, lifestyle and CPG businesses specifically — so we already understand counter operations, shop-in-shop economics, and seasonal stock cycles before day one.

Scale that's actually been proven

Our systems run across thousands of storefronts and multiple currencies, languages and regulatory environments — not just a handful of pilot sites.

A support model built on SLAs, not tickets

Structured severity levels, defined escalation paths and proactive monitoring — store-first prioritisation instead of a generic help-desk queue.

On-the-ground, in your language

Local teams across Singapore, Indonesia and Thailand mean support in local languages and time zones — not an overseas call centre reading from a script.

Best-of-breed, not locked-in

We partner with leading software vendors and add our own integration and AI layer on top — so you get proven, independently-supported software rather than a single vendor's closed ecosystem.

We integrate, we don't just install

Every system we deploy is connected — POS, CRM, inventory, ERP and e-commerce speak to each other from day one, instead of becoming another disconnected silo.

Integrated Retail — advanced, unified retail systems for fashion, lifestyle, F&B and CPG businesses across Asia Pacific, from a partner who integrates and builds: POS, CRM, e-commerce, inventory, analytics and AI, as one.
