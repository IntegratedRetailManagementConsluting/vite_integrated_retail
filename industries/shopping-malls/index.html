Turn every visit, tenant and zone into a more profitable decision.

The Business Challenge

Shopping malls are not short of footfall. They are short of connected visibility.

Tenant sales visibility

Mall operators often can’t see tenant-level sales and performance in one place.

Footfall vs. conversion by zone

Traffic is tracked, but it’s unclear which zones and levels actually convert.

Fragmented tenant systems

Every tenant runs its own systems, making mall-wide reporting difficult.

Dwell-time & layout optimisation

It’s hard to know which zones hold shoppers longest and why.

Marketing spend attribution

Connecting campaigns and events to actual footfall and tenant sales is difficult.

What Mall Management Actually Cares About

Five lenses on the same connected data.

How footfall, tenant and traffic data get used across the concerns that actually drive mall management decisions.

How It’s Used

Brings zone-level footfall and conversion data into rent-review and renewal conversations, so asking rates are backed by measured traffic rather than the tenant’s word against the landlord’s.

Business Outcome

A defensible, data-backed position at every rent review — for units earning their space and units that aren’t.

Illustrative Impact

Malls that bring footfall data into renewal negotiations typically reach a stronger position at the table and catch under-performing units earlier in the lease cycle. Directional, illustrative — not a guaranteed figure.

How It’s Used

Measures whether a campaign or event actually moved footfall and dwell time — not just impressions or attendance — and breaks that down by zone and day-part.

Business Outcome

Marketing spend justified and directed by measured footfall impact, not assumption.

Illustrative Impact

Example: reallocating part of a quarter’s event budget toward the zones and day-parts shown to actually respond to it. Illustrative, not a committed figure.

How It’s Used

Compares footfall, occupancy and tenant sales trends across zones — or across multiple malls in a portfolio — in one dashboard instead of collating separate reports.

Business Outcome

A single, current view of which assets, zones and tenant categories are genuinely earning their space.

Illustrative Impact

Example: spotting one consistently under-performing zone or asset within a single quarterly review, ahead of the next lease-renewal cycle. Illustrative example, not one customer’s reported result.

How It’s Used

Uses real-time and historical traffic data to plan peak-hour staffing, queue management and crowd-density monitoring across entrances, escalators and common areas.

Business Outcome

Smoother peak-period operations and a documented basis for staffing and crowd-management decisions.

Illustrative Impact

Example: adjusting weekend staffing rosters around measured hourly footfall peaks instead of a flat schedule. Illustrative example.

How It’s Used

Traces how visitors actually move through the mall — entrance to zone to dwell time to exit — to see where the journey breaks down between arriving and spending.

Business Outcome

Layout, wayfinding and tenant-placement decisions grounded in how shoppers actually move, not assumption.

Illustrative Impact

Example: identifying a high-footfall zone with unusually short dwell time, pointing to a layout or tenant-mix issue rather than a traffic problem. Illustrative example.

The Core Solution

One system built around tenant-mix decisions: FootfallCam.

For shopping malls, the single highest-leverage platform is traffic and location analytics. Individual tenants may run their own POS, CRM or inventory systems — but tenant-mix strategy itself runs on footfall data.

  • Mall-wide and zone-by-zone footfall counting, with entrance, escalator and common-area coverage
  • Footfall-to-conversion analysis by zone, level and tenant category
  • Dwell-time and visitor-path insight to see where shoppers actually spend time
  • Queue and crowd-density monitoring for peak-hour planning
  • Campaign and event impact measured against actual traffic, not just impressions

Where a tenant also needs its own POS, CRM or unified-commerce systems, Integrated Retail supports that too — but for the mall operator itself, FootfallCam is the platform this page is built around.
