Food & Beverage Retail

See every shift. Act before revenue is lost.

F&B operators lose revenue to walkaways, idle tables, slow service and inconsistent execution — hidden behind the final sales number. Integrated Retail’s Restaurant Operating System turns guest flow, service and SOP signals into the next best action, for every outlet and every shift.

The Business Challenge

F&B retail is not short of guests. It is short of visibility into what happens once they walk in.

Live floor issues go unseen

Queues, idle tables and food-ready gaps stay invisible until a guest complaint or a sales dip — too late for the shift team to act.

Service delays compound

From queue to kitchen to pickup, small delays across the shift add up to lost throughput exactly when it matters most.

SOP execution drifts outlet by outlet

Without a shared standard, packing accuracy, patrols and hygiene routines vary — and manual audits catch it too late.

Marketing spend without proof

It’s hard to tell which campaigns actually created visits and sales, versus just impressions.

No fair way to benchmark outlets

Comparing outlets by total sales alone hides which shifts, staff or practices are actually driving performance.

The Core Solution

One system built around every shift: FootfallCam Restaurant Operating System.

For F&B, the single highest-leverage platform isn’t the point of sale alone — it’s what happens on the floor between order and service. Restaurant OS turns guest flow, queues, table turnover and service execution into a live operating view for every outlet.

  • Live store operations — queue, food-readiness and table status surfaced as the next best action
  • Service speed tracked end-to-end: queue, seat-to-order, prep and order-to-serve
  • SOP compliance across packing accuracy, patrols, food availability and hygiene, by outlet
  • Marketing ROI — which campaigns create real traffic and sales uplift, not just impressions
  • National benchmarking to replicate top outlets and diagnose the KPIs holding others back

Where a chain also runs Cegid Retail or another POS, Integrated Retail connects that too — but for day-to-day F&B operations, FootfallCam’s Restaurant OS is the platform this page is built around.
