Health & Beauty Retail

Turn every counter, sample and customer into a more loyal relationship.

Health and beauty retailers balance high-touch counter service with fast-moving stock, testers and expiry dates. Integrated Retail connects counters, CRM and inventory so every customer gets consistent, personalised service, wherever they shop.

Talk to a Retail Consultant → Explore Health & Beauty Solutions Request the Brochure
The Business Challenge

Health and beauty retail is not short of products. It is short of consistent service.

High-touch service under time pressure

Counter staff need product and customer history at their fingertips to advise well, fast.

Testers & sample stock control

Testers, samples and gifts-with-purchase are hard to track separately from sellable stock.

Fragmented loyalty across counters

The same customer looks like a stranger at every different counter, store or channel.

Expiry-dated inventory risk

Cosmetics and skincare carry shelf lives — slow-moving stock becomes write-off risk.

Consistency of brand experience

Service and merchandising quality varies between counters, standalone stores and concessions.
