Fashion & Apparel Retail

Turn every store, customer and SKU into a more profitable decision.

Fashion retailers manage seasonal demand, high SKU complexity, rapidly changing consumer journeys and fragmented channels. Integrated Retail connects stores, products, people and data so you can make faster, smarter decisions and deliver stronger retail performance.

The Business Challenge

Fashion retail is not short of data. It is short of connected decisions.

High inventory, still missing the sale

You have inventory, but customers still can’t find what they want.

Sales decline, unclear root cause

Sales are down, but it’s not clear if it’s traffic, conversion or basket size.

Fragmented omnichannel customer journeys

Customers shop across stores, online and mobile, but data is fragmented.

Reactive markdown management

You’re forced into late markdowns, eroding margins.

Store execution varies by location

Performance and customer experience are inconsistent across stores.

Solution: Cegid POS, WMS & inventory, CRM, omnichannel and shop-in-shop deployment, plus RFID-based stock accuracy, integrated with SAP.

Outcome: 550+ stores running on one connected system across Indonesia, and continuing to expand.
