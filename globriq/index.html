Home / Products / Globriq

One governed pipeline for every system you run.

Globriq is our enterprise integration and digital operations platform — built for retailers running POS, ERP, WMS, CRM, e-commerce and supplier networks that were never designed to talk to each other. It acquires data from any system, transforms it to any target format, and delivers it reliably, with automation, monitoring and governance built in.

Why Globriq Exists

Most retail integrations are built one pair at a time, by hand

Large retail organisations run dozens of systems. Most were connected point-to-point, on someone's laptop, on a schedule only they remember. That approach doesn't scale — and when it breaks, it breaks quietly.

Disconnected systems

Data sits siloed across POS, ERP, WMS and CRM, with no shared source of truth.

Manual processes

Files are moved, renamed and reconciled by hand, on schedules people have to remember.

Point-to-point sprawl

Every new link is bespoke — N systems trend toward N² brittle connections.

No visibility

Failures surface days later — usually as a customer complaint or a finance discrepancy.

High operational cost

Skilled engineers spend their week firefighting transfers instead of building.

Fragile & unauditable

No single record of what moved, when, or whether it actually succeeded.

What Globriq Does

Acquire. Transform. Deliver.

One data path, applied consistently across every connected system — with automation, monitoring and governance spanning every stage.

Acquire

Pull or receive data over FTP, SFTP, Azure Blob, APIs and databases — from Cegid Retail, SAP, Oracle, MS Dynamics and more.

Transform

Map fields and render CSV, TXT, JSON or fixed-width to any target schema — reusable templates, not one-off scripts.

Deliver

Push to destination systems with retries, idempotency and confirmation — nothing is delivered twice, nothing is silently dropped.

Hover or tap a step for details.

Orchestrate

Scheduling, retries, queues and workers — across every stage.

Monitor

Dashboards, events, health checks and a full audit trail — across every stage.

Govern

RBAC, tenant isolation and scoped credentials — across every stage.

Engineered, Not Scripted

Platform principles enforced in the runtime

These aren't slogans — they're checked on every request.

  • Fail Closed. On doubt, deny. Errors never expose data or skip a control.
  • Security First. Security is a design input, not a later hardening pass.
  • Observability First. Every action is measured, logged and traceable by default.
  • Idempotency. Re-running a job never duplicates or corrupts data.
  • Resilience. Failures are expected, isolated and recovered automatically.
  • Auditability. Who did what, and when — reconstructable end to end.
  • Multi-Tenant. Tenants are isolated in data, configuration and execution.
  • Cloud Native. Stateless, containerized, and horizontally scaled on AWS.
