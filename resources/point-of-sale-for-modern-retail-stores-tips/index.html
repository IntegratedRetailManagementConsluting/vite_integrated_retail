5 Tips for Smarter Point of Sale for Modern Retail Stores

Blog 16 September 2025 • Integrated Retail • 3 min read

Point of Sale for Modern Retail Stores – Singapore’s luxury goods market is valued at USD 10.45 billion in 2025 and projected to reach USD 14.26 billion by 2030 (Mordor Intelligence). The city also ranked 5th globally for new luxury store openings in 2024 (Singapore Business Review). At the same time, tourists contributed S$3.9 billion in retail spending in Jan–Sep 2024 (Retail Asia).

For multi‑store fashion, lifestyle, and luxury retailers, this growth means opportunity, but only if operations, customer experience, and data scale with it. That’s where a modern point of sale for retail stores in Singapore, powered by Cegid Retail by Integrated Retail, comes in. 

Here are five ways to make your POS work smarter.

1. Choose a Scalable POS for Growth

With Singapore among the world’s leaders in new luxury store openings, growth is not slowing down. A cloud‑native POS ensures brands can expand into new malls, airports, or regional markets without disruption. For example, a fashion chain rolling out stores across Orchard, Marina Bay, and suburban malls can maintain unified operations while preserving a consistent brand experience at every touchpoint.

2. Deliver Seamless Omnichannel Experiences

Singaporean shoppers are already omnichannel: 68.3% use both online and in‑store channels, and omnichannel spending makes up over half of local retail expenditure (IMDA). With a smart POS, services such as buy‑online‑pick‑up‑in‑store (BOPIS), cross‑store returns, and endless aisle become frictionless. Cegid Retail unifies CRM and clienteling tools, enabling luxury retailers to deliver personalised service across channels.

3. Keep Stock Accurate Across Locations

Retail customers expect availability, whether it’s a specific handbag at Marina Bay Sands or sneakers in VivoCity. Inventory complexity is magnified in fashion and lifestyle with sizes, colours, and seasonal SKUs. With a unified retail POS system in Singapore, retailers gain real‑time visibility across all stores and channels, plus mobile POS for in‑aisle selling. This ensures customers always get the products they want, where they want them.

4. Use Insights to Guide Store Decisions

Informed decisions separate retailers who thrive from those who stagnate. Cegid Retail delivers AI-powered dashboards and real-time insights into store and product performance, enabling managers to react quickly to trends. Whether it’s a handbag selling out in one branch or slow-moving trainers elsewhere, retailers can respond with confidence.

5. Streamline Operations Across Stores

Operational excellence requires more than just sales processing. Mobile apps connected to the point of sale for retail stores simplify task management, employee training, and daily activities. Cegid’s Operational Excellence platform consolidates planning, communication, and team engagement into a single app. With AI assistance, tasks flow seamlessly from head office to shop floor, freeing staff to focus on customers instead of admin.

Stay Ready for the Future of Retail

Luxury and fashion retail in Singapore isn’t slowing: total spending is projected to grow to S$14.7 billion by 2025, driven by both affluent locals and international shoppers. With younger under‑40 consumers driving demand for personalised, premium experiences, future‑ready POS systems are the backbone of customer engagement.

Integrated Retail makes Cegid the solution of choice: embedding advanced AI, predictive insights, and scalability to ensure multi‑store retailers aren’t just keeping up; they’re leading.

Final Takeaway

The right point of sale for retail stores empowers growth, improves customer satisfaction, and keeps operations efficient. Making the wrong choice can slow down at a time when the industry is accelerating. For multi‑store fashion and luxury brands, the right POS system is the difference between simply operating and truly excelling. Contact us to evaluate and adopt Cegid Retail for your business.
