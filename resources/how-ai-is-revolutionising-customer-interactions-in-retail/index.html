The Rise of AI in Retail

The retail industry is undergoing a major transformation. Today, customers expect speed, convenience, and personalisation, and traditional methods can no longer keep up. One solution that’s making a big impact is AI-driven customer interaction in retail.

AI enables stores to engage with customers more effectively, much like having a digital sales representative available 24/7. According to Desk365, 35% of businesses already use AI to enhance customer interactions, while 42% plan to adopt it soon. For retailers, this isn’t just a trend. It’s becoming a necessity.[1]

How AI Customer Interaction in Retail Works

Many retail business owners ask: “How does shopping use AI?” or “How are customers interacting with it in stores?” The truth is, AI has moved from buzzword to everyday reality in retail, reshaping how stores engage with shoppers. In fact, how AI is used in retail stores today shows just how seamlessly technology supports both businesses and shoppers. 

Here are some practical examples:

1. Scan a QR Code

One of the simplest ways to see how shopping uses AI is through QR codes. By scanning them in-store, customers are directed to AI-powered shopping assistants. No app download is needed. Just a quick scan to access instant support.

2. Browse Product & Ask Question

After the QR code is scanned, customers can see a catalogue and browse the products in it. They can also ask any questions regarding products in the stores. AI will answer it for them. The duties are similar to those of sales representatives in general, the difference is only digital-based, not physical. 

3. Get Product Recommendations, Promotions, and Discounts

To enrich the shopping journey, AI suggests products tailored to customer needs, facilitates cross-selling, and shares details about current promotions or loyalty rewards. This is a clear example of how consumers are using AI in retail stores to make smarter and more rewarding purchase decisions.

How Multi-store Businesses Benefit from AI in Retail

AI-driven customer interaction offers several tangible benefits for large retail businesses:

1. Increase Customer Satisfaction

Many retail operators ask, “Does AI really increase customer satisfaction?” The answer is a resounding yes. By providing instant support, personalised guidance, and seamless assistance, AI helps customers shop more easily and efficiently, addressing their needs in real time.

2. Boost Effectivity

Studies show that AI customer interaction can handle 13.8% more customer inquiries per hour.[2] This streamlines operations, reducing reliance on large sales teams while maintaining high service quality. 

3. Increase Sales

AI can suggest products, facilitate cross-selling, and highlight promotions, increasing the conversion rates by 35% on average.[3] This applies not only to offline stores but also online platforms, making it ideal for multi-store retail operations.

The Future of Retail Belongs to AI

The question for retailers today is not “Should we adopt AI?” but “How fast can we leverage it to stay competitive?”
