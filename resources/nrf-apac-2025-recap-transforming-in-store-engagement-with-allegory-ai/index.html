NRF APAC 2025 Recap: Transforming In-Store Engagement with Allegory AI

Allegory 17 June 2025 • Integrated Retail • 6 min read

The recently concluded NRF APAC 2025 event at Marina Bay Sands was a resounding success, bringing together some of the most forward-thinking retail technology providers and retail brands across the Asia-Pacific region. Integrated Retail was proud to take part in this landmark exhibition, where we had the opportunity to showcase our latest innovations—most notably, the launch of Allegory AI, our intelligent product storytelling platform designed to bridge the digital-physical divide in brick-and-mortar retail.

This blog post is part of our NRF APAC 2025 recap series, and in this instalment, we focus on how Allegory AI captured the attention of retailers by offering a new way to enhance the in-store customer journey.

Introducing Allegory AI: A New Paradigm in Product Storytelling

Allegory AI is a next-generation, in-store product storytelling solution that empowers retailers to offer rich, informative, and interactive experiences for their shoppers—without requiring additional frontline manpower.

Using Allegory AI, customers can access detailed product information, personalized guidance, and real-time assistance directly from their mobile device, simply by scanning a QR code displayed next to an item in-store. From there, they are seamlessly directed to the Allegory AI native app—no downloads or installations required.

Once in the application:

  • Customers may hover over product tags or QR markers to retrieve dynamic product details curated by the retailer.
  • They can engage with an intelligent, in-built AI assistant, which responds to product-related queries instantly and conversationally.
  • The solution acts as a digital sales assistant, available 24/7, consistently delivering tailored responses based on the store’s official product data.

    NRF Attendees looking at Allegory AI

Elevating the Art of Storytelling: From Data to Experience

One of the most powerful aspects of Allegory AI lies in its ability to move beyond static product facts—and unlock immersive, next-level storytelling.

In an age where consumers increasingly seek meaningful connections with the products they buy, Allegory AI transforms each item on the shelf into a compelling narrative. Whether the shopper is buying a luxury dress, a serum for sensitive skin, or organic free-range eggs, the solution provides a platform to tell the story behind the product—bringing emotional context to every transaction.

Here are just a few examples of how Allegory AI can bring storytelling to life in different retail segments:

  • Apparel: Imagine a customer scanning a dress and seeing a video or AR avatar of the designer explaining their inspiration behind the piece, the cultural or artistic influences, where the fabric was sourced, and how it was ethically produced. This deepens appreciation, fosters brand loyalty, and transforms fashion from commodity to craftsmanship.
  • Skincare: A shopper browsing facial cleansers could engage with a virtual skincare clinician who explains the science behind the formulation, how it benefits different skin types, and how to incorporate it into a broader routine. This empowers more confident purchases and bridges the gap between clinical advice and self-service retail.
  • Sustainable Food: For ethically-minded consumers, Allegory AI can offer a window into the product’s journey—from the name of the farm where the eggs were sourced, to the conditions the animals were raised in, to certifications for organic practices. This type of storytelling builds transparency and trust, making every label come alive.

With this capability, Allegory AI not only answers questions—it enriches the shopping experience through authenticity and narrative, something no static shelf label or website description can do alone.

In essence, Allegory AI doesn’t just display product information—it creates a moment of connection. Every product becomes a chapter. Every aisle becomes a curated story. And the store itself becomes an immersive brand experience.

Our Live Demonstration at NRF APAC

To effectively communicate the value of Allegory AI, we created an immersive, live demonstration at our booth. The setup simulated a typical grocery aisle using a selection of curated items from German Market Place and a range of wines to illustrate the breadth of product categories the solution could support.

Visitors were able to interact with Allegory AI as real customers would. The demo showcased the solution’s versatility in answering a wide range of customer questions, such as:

  • “Is this product packaged sustainably?”
  • “Does this item contain common allergens like dairy or nuts?”
  • “What kind of wine pairs well with dark chocolate?”

Whether the question was fact-based or preference-driven, Allegory AI was able to respond with relevant and accurate information—instantly.

This level of personalization and transparency would be difficult for in-store associates to replicate consistently, particularly in high-SKU environments such as grocery, wine, personal care, or specialty retail. Allegory AI solves this challenge by offering contextual, easily accessible product knowledge at scale.

How It Works: Connecting to Your Existing Retail Systems

A common question from NRF attendees was, “Where does Allegory AI source its information?”

The answer is simple: Allegory AI draws product data directly from a retailer’s existing Product Information Management (PIM) system, inventory management platform, or backend database. This ensures that the product information being presented to customers is:

  • Accurate and up-to-date
  • Consistent across channels
  • Tailored to the store’s exact assortment and policies

This integration also allows Allegory AI to reflect inventory availability per location, accommodate regional product variations, and display attributes such as certifications, sustainability scores, origin stories, and customer ratings—if provided.

Why Allegory AI Matters in 2025 and Beyond

As retail increasingly shifts toward experience-first models, Allegory AI offers a strategic advantage for physical stores seeking to replicate the seamless, information-rich nature of eCommerce—without the complexity of deploying mobile apps or hiring more floor staff.

With Allegory AI, retailers can:

  • Support informed decision-making for values-driven, health-conscious, and curious shoppers
  • Reduce reliance on overburdened sales associates and save time spent on training them
  • Enhance shopper confidence and brand trust through product transparency
  • Improve in-store engagement metrics, time spent per product, and conversion rates
  • Get insights into the customer journey

This is not just a tech demonstration—it is a tangible step toward digitizing the store floor in a customer-centric, scalable, and cost-effective way.

What’s Next for Allegory AI

The response from NRF APAC attendees was overwhelmingly positive. Many retailers—especially those in grocery, specialty food, cosmetics, fashion and wine—expressed keen interest in piloting the solution in their flagship stores. Integrated Retail is currently in discussions with several partners to roll out Allegory AI pilots in the coming months across Southeast Asia.

We look forward to sharing more about these partnerships and results in future updates.
