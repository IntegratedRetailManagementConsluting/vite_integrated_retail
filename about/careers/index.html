IT Infrastructure Executive

Singapore · Full-time

Own the design, installation and day-to-day health of our network infrastructure and IT assets — servers, security and connectivity, end to end.

View full description

Responsibilities

  • Manage the network asset inventory, lifecycle and documentation, owning issues through to resolution
  • Coordinate installation of in-house and remote hardware/software across the network, and manage e-mail, print and backup servers
  • Maintain security solutions (firewalls, anti-virus, intrusion detection) and administer user accounts and permissions in Active Directory
  • Test and report on network and server performance, and develop infrastructure maintenance strategies
  • Research and recommend network products, services, protocols and standards; negotiate with vendors and contractors

What We’re Looking For

  • 3+ years’ experience managing and configuring enterprise LANs, WANs, WLANs and VPNs
  • Working knowledge of Active Directory, Group Policies, MS Exchange, core switching/routing, SSL/IPSec, SAN, virtualization, business continuity and disaster recovery
  • Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, Microsoft, IBM) is a strong plus
  • Conversant with .Net or PHP; experience installing network cabling and telephony systems
